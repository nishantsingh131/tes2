FAQ

Before you buy

How long do I have access to a series?

Access to a single series runs until that exam's scheduled date. All-Access runs for a full year from purchase.

Are the tests in English and Hindi?

Every series is available in English, and most State PSC and Railway series are also available in Hindi.

Can I retake a test after submitting it?

Yes. You can reattempt any test as many times as you like — each attempt is scored and ranked separately.

What if a test doesn't load or I face a payment issue?

Write to our support team from the Help Centre and we'll resolve it, or refund the purchase, within 48 hours.

Is there a free test before I buy?

Yes. Each exam category has one free full-length test so you can check the format before buying the series.

Do the tests follow the latest exam pattern?

Every series is reviewed against the most recent official notification for that exam, including any pattern or marking changes.

Can I attempt tests on my phone?

Yes. The test interface works in any phone, tablet or desktop browser — no app installation needed.

Will I get a payment receipt?

Yes, a receipt for your purchase is emailed to you automatically right after payment.
